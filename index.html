About me

I am Arushi Kabra, a student of finance, business, and disciplined thinking.

I am currently pursuing CA (Chartered Accountancy), CMA (Cost & Management Accountancy), and B.Com. I am deeply interested in business strategy, finance, accounting, economics, technology, entrepreneurship, and decision-making.

I believe learning goes beyond textbooks. I enjoy studying real companies, reading articles, researching industries, and understanding why businesses succeed or fail.

This website is my digital knowledge portfolio where I document my research, analyses, case studies, reflections, and continuous learning journey. My goal is not just to earn professional qualifications, but to become someone who understands businesses from multiple perspectives and can simplify complex ideas.

I value curiosity, discipline, critical thinking, and continuous improvement. I believe asking better questions leads to better decisions.
