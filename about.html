About

I am building a long-term understanding of how the world works.

I am Arushi Kabra, a CA and CMA student, and a third-year B.Com student at Mumbai University. I am driven by a deep interest in business strategy, finance, accounting, economics, technology, entrepreneurship, and the discipline of decision-making.

Why I do this

I have always been drawn to the way businesses are shaped by incentives, systems, people, and timing. I am interested not only in what companies do, but in why they do it, how they create value, and why some succeed while others fail. That curiosity has led me to study real companies, read widely, analyze industries, and build a habit of asking better questions.

For me, learning is not limited to textbooks or formal qualifications. It is something that happens through observation, research, reflection, and lived engagement with the world. That is why this website exists: as a digital knowledge portfolio where I document my research, analyses, case studies, and reflections in a way that is both practical and intellectually grounded.

How I approach learning

I believe the goal is not simply to accumulate knowledge, but to develop the ability to understand complexity clearly. I value curiosity, discipline, critical thinking, and the willingness to revise assumptions when new evidence appears. I am interested in simplifying complex ideas without reducing them to superficial explanations.

My aim is not only to earn professional qualifications, but to become someone who can understand businesses from multiple perspectives and contribute meaningfully to the finance and business ecosystem. In the future, I hope to build ventures, contribute to meaningful conversations in education and finance, and help others understand these subjects in a more practical and thoughtful way.

This website will continue to grow as I learn, write, and publish more research. I am interested in building something lasting: a place where ideas can be examined carefully, connected across disciplines, and shared with others who value thoughtful thinking.
